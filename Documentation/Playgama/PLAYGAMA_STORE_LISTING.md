Hold and drag to aim and fire from the planet's surface. Rock meteors must die before they hit: every impact costs a
life. Shining gold stars are different. Let them land and they are yours; shoot one and it is gone.

Every level-up stops time and offers three upgrades for the gold you saved: Rapid Fire, Double Shot, Piercing,
Explosions, Freeze Pulse, an Orbital Drone or a charged Laser Beam. Spend it now for power, or keep it: unspent gold
goes to your bank and unlocks new worlds.

- 8 real planets of the Solar System, each with its own lives and passive power
- 5 meteor types: fast darts, armored iron rocks, splitters and giant boulders
- 8 run upgrades and a combo multiplier for skilled streaks
- Endless waves that keep getting harder: how long can you hold out?
- Global leaderboard for your best score

## How to play (EN)
- **Aim and shoot:** hold and drag on the screen (touch) or hold the left mouse button (desktop). The blaster fires toward your finger or cursor.
- **Meteors:** destroy them before they hit your planet. Each hit costs one life.
- **Gold:** don't shoot it. Gold that reaches the planet is added to your session gold.
- **Level-up:** buy one of three upgrades with session gold, or press Skip.
- **Pause:** pause button (top right) or **P** on the keyboard.
